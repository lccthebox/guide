Mobile Guide

Manage attendance, Korean tutoring, absences, and learning records on your phone.

Open Native Teacher Lounge
AttendanceKorean tutoringLearning records

Follow these steps

1

Sign in

Open Lounge → Enter registered information

  1. Enter your registered name, phone number, and 8-digit date of birth.
  2. Your name is not case-sensitive. You may use your first name or registered Korean name.
  3. If no record is found, send your information to THE BOX so we can register it.
2

Join a session

Schedule → Select a date → Join this session

  1. Choose Morning, Evening, or the single Weekend session.
  2. Add an optional message for the team.
  3. Tap Register attendance and check the calendar status.
3

Request Korean tutoring

Join this session → I would like Korean tutoring

  1. Turn on the Korean tutoring option.
  2. Check the updated start time shown on the button.
  3. Arrive one hour before the regular conversation session.
4

Report an absence

Schedule → Select a date → Unable to join

  1. Select the regular study time you cannot attend.
  2. Enter a reason. This field is required.
  3. Tap Report absence. A substitute is not required for a native absence.
5

Check goals and lesson records

Learning goal / My records

  1. Review your Korean level, goal, and target date.
  2. Check curriculum progress and feedback saved by your tutor.
  3. Use My Page to confirm your regular schedule and profile.
Korean tutoring start times: Weekday morning 10:00 AM, weekday evening 7:00 PM, and weekend 12:00 PM. Regular conversation starts one hour later.
Open the link in Chrome or Safari and add it to your Home Screen. Refresh when the Lounge shows that new information is available.
